Thesis: wood vinegar and rice salt stress

MSc, Sylhet Agricultural University, 2023–2025

Plant metabolites against Acinetobacter baumannii

Computational protein–ligand screening and molecular dynamics analysis. Published in Informatics in Medicine Unlocked.

Garlic and lead toxicity in mice

In vivo study of garlic as a treatment for dose-dependent sub-acute lead toxicity in Swiss-Albino mice. I handled animal grouping, dosing, monitoring and sample collection, and assisted with hematological and biochemical analysis.

Probiotic lactic acid bacteria from chicken gut

Isolated lactic acid bacteria and tested their probiotic effect on growth and health in Sonali chickens. Methods: bacterial culture, microscopy, biochemical identification, DNA extraction, PCR and gel electrophoresis.
